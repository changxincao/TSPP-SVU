# Distribution and volatility inputs

Four experimental settings form a 2×2 comparison: Normal versus Lognormal, crossed with lane CV parameter [0.1,0.3] versus [0.4,0.6]. Each setting uses the same five master seeds, 20261021–20261025, I=15 carriers, J=50 lanes, H=75 historical observations, 40 randomly sampled decision contexts and 1,000 conditional OOS demand realizations per context. Common loadings remain in [0.3,0.5], and selection bounds are [2,12].

For corresponding instances across the four settings, procurement parameters, demand coefficients, historical covariates and decision contexts are unchanged. The residual distribution and CV-parameter interval vary.

Training and validation windows follow the main experiment.
